Safety first

  • Treat every firearm as if it's loaded.
  • Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction.
  • Keep your finger off the trigger until you're on target.
  • Know your target and what's beyond it.

Set the phone up before you start, and don't handle it with a firearm in your hand. Turn on auto-stop in Settings so the recording ends on its own. No time is worth a safety mistake.
